QR Inventory System

Inventory · QR Codes · Web Application

CONCEPT

Connecting physical assets to digital information.

This project explores a QR-based workflow for making physical inventory easier to identify and track through a web application.

The core idea is simple: scan an item, identify its digital record, view its information, update its status, and keep the activity connected to the inventory system.
